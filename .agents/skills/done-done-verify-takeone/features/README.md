# takeone features

The repo's maintained verification map; a proof that drives one entry point is incomplete when this lists others.

- [Render a synthetic take to a framed demo MP4 offline](render-a-synthetic-take-to-a-framed-demo.md): Run the README quick start from a git checkout: install dependencies, then generate the demo take (`scripts/synth-take.ts`)
- [Plan and render a recorded take](plan-and-render-a-recorded-take.md): `takeone make <id> --no-ai` on the realistic board scene: perception, beats, camera and render, judged from whole frames of the film (`src/make.ts`, `src/perceive/`, `src/beats/`, `src/camera/`)
- [Pick one of three directions and polish it to 9/10 on the board](pick-a-direction-and-polish-it-to-nine.md): `takeone directions <take> --board`, `takeone pick`, `takeone polish`: three rendered cuts judged once each, the owner's pick, then judge-refine rounds to 9/10, every option and round playable on the sign-off board (`src/directions/`, `src/polish/`, `src/board/`)
- [Plan a take with the AI planner](plan-a-take-with-the-ai-planner.md): `takeone make <id>` with a key, a picked account or a signed-in CLI; the free preflight refusal, and a billed run only with approval (`src/decide/`)
- [Change the look and shape of a film on rerender](change-the-look-and-shape-on-rerender.md): `takeone render <take> --theme`, `--set`, `--aspect`, `--resolution`, `--format`, and `take.json` cuts, speed and zooms (`src/themes.ts`, `src/render/edits.ts`)
- [Add keycaps, spotlights and blur to a take](add-keycaps-spotlights-and-blur.md): shortcut keycaps from recorded combos, `take.json` spotlight, blur and toast windows (`src/render/overlays.ts`)
- [Blur the camera moves](blur-the-camera-moves.md): the default half-frame shutter on fast camera moves, `--set motion_blur` (`src/render/motion-blur.ts`)
- [Render a fast preview of the same film](render-a-fast-preview-of-the-same-film.md): `takeone render <take> --preview` renders the same camera path at 30 fps with the short edge at most 500 px (864x486 for 1080p), from a proxy no larger than 1080p; `camera.json` must stay byte-identical to the full render's
- [Prove a plan edit follows its beat across a re-cut](prove-a-plan-edit-follows-its-beat-across-a-re-cut.md): Edit `analysis/plan.json` by hand, trim the take, run `make` again, and assert the edit lands on the same beat key at its new id (`src/beats/key.ts`, `src/decide/plan.ts`)
- [Prove the camera drag ease on a real take](prove-the-camera-drag-ease-on-a-real-take.md): Render a real take with a drag, then measure the camera bars and the per-frame text cuts, and keep the dragged card and cursor in view through a trim or cut across the drag, wide and portrait (`src/camera/gesture.ts`, `src/render/edits.ts`, `scripts/drag-audit.ts`)
- [Prove portrait, square and 4:5 exports re-aim on every act](prove-portrait-square-and-4-5-exports-re-aim-on-every-act.md): Render the board scene take with `--aspect portrait|square|4:5` and measure every click, typing, scroll and drag sample against the shown crop (`scripts/verification/act-framing.ts`, `src/camera/solver.ts`)
- [Prove a manual zoom arrives and holds on every export shape](prove-a-manual-zoom-arrives-and-holds-on-every-shape.md): Render the board scene at its own portrait and square shape (and as a phone take) with `take.json` zooms and measure arrival, hold and the move gates on the shown window (`scripts/verification/manual-zoom.ts`)
- [Prove a phone take frames each tapped control](features/prove-a-phone-take-frames-each-tapped-control.md): Record the Tidewater fixture on your own Android emulator, render portrait and wide, and check each tap sits inside the shown view with nothing cut
- [Edit a zoom and reuse a saved look without keyframes](edit-a-zoom-and-reuse-a-saved-look.md): `takeone zoom <take> list|add|move|remove` edits `take.json` zooms and `takeone look save|list|show|remove` + `render --look` reuse a named theme, aspect and `--set` set; stills from a real board render, before and after (`src/refine.ts`)
- [Size, hide and loop the drawn cursor on a real take](size-hide-and-loop-the-drawn-cursor.md): Render a cursor-free take with `cursor_size`, `cursor_hide_idle_s` and `cursor_loop`, then measure size, fades, loop end and click arrival from the encoded film (`scripts/cursor-audit.ts`)
- [Prove titles and captions stay off the app in every export shape on a real take](prove-captions-clear-toasts-on-a-real-take.md): Capture the headless board and its portrait layout as a phone take, add a title and captions, render every shape, theme, a long caption and a 320x180 export, and read `covers` in `safe-area.json` (`src/render/stage.ts`, `src/render/render.ts`)
- [Make a motion film from screens](make-a-motion-film-from-screens.md): `takeone motion <image|page|url>...` plans a storyboard locally and renders it; `takeone render <motion-dir> --aspect` re-lays it (`src/motion/`, `resources/motion/`)
- [Frame a recording with model-made motion segments](frame-a-recording-with-model-made-segments.md): `takeone motion film <video> --intro/--callout/--outro` (`src/motion/film.ts`, `src/motion/segment.ts`)
- [Record a take](record-a-take.md): `takeone doctor`, `takeone record` (desktop, `--android`, `--ios-sim`), `stop` and `list`, only on dedicated fixtures (`src/record.ts`, `src/android/`, `src/ios/`)
- [Plan a capture with the user's account](plan-a-capture-with-the-users-account.md): `takeone capture make <take> --planner-account` plans through the signed-in account `takeone make` resolves, and stops with `planner-unavailable` instead of planning with local rules when there is none
- [Print the installed version](print-the-installed-version.md): Run the `version` journey: `takeone --version` and `-v` against package.json, under a node-only PATH
- [Check the rendered films visually](check-the-rendered-offline-mp4-visually.md): the visual check on one film, and the `visual-reference`, `visual`, `visual-controls` and `visual-design` journeys across ten shape and theme cells (`scripts/verification/`)
- [Drive the Tidewater board in a browser through the staged scene](drive-the-tidewater-board-in-a-browser-t.md): Open scripts/e2e/scene.html directly as a file:// page; it is the staged Tidewater board used by the e2e takes (`scripts/e2e/scene.html`)
- [Run the output quality gates on real and synthetic takes](run-the-output-quality-gates.md): `npm run quality` gates three fixtures, including the real-UI headless scene, with every §4 metric and the all-frame text-cut audit, and prints `passed` only when every goal is met (`scripts/quality.ts`, `scripts/cut-audit.ts`)

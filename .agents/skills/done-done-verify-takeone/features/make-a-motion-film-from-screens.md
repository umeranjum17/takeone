# Make a motion film from screens

Part of the takeone verification map. Source: `src/motion/`, `src/motion/hyperframes.ts`, `resources/motion/`, `resources/fonts/`, `resources/demo/`, `src/themes.ts`, `scripts/motion-proof.ts`, `scripts/motion-gates.ts`, `scripts/motion-edge.ts`, `scripts/motion-edge-proof.ts`, `scripts/motion-beat-proof.ts`, `scripts/motion-fragment-proof.ts`, `scripts/motion-determinism.ts`, `test/motion-hyperframes.test.ts`. Full recipe and gotchas: `docs/verification/motion-films.md`, `docs/motion.md`.

## How to get to it (user POV)

The user has screenshots, an HTML page or a URL and wants a launch film without recording: `takeone motion design.png --out <dir> --title "Ship your next idea" --device browser`. It writes a take-shaped folder (`take.json`, `sources/`, `storyboard.json`, `out/<id>.mp4`, `render.json`) through the default HyperFrames engine; `takeone render <dir> --engine legacy --aspect portrait` re-lays the same timeline and `--stills 1.5,8.5` writes PNGs (the HyperFrames path rejects `--aspect` and `--stills`). With three or more screens and no `--pattern` the local rules plan a launch film (kinetic type, one beat per labelled screen and the end card); the bento recap needs at least three screens labelled with `--region ...:label`. No screenshot is sent to a planner.

## Driving it

Harness: Shell (bash), ffmpeg/ffprobe, `fm-mem-gate.sh` for every render. Needs the pinned HyperFrames pair (`node bin/takeone.mjs motion install-hyperframes` once; network + npm) for the default path, and the pinned headless shell (`node bin/takeone.mjs motion install-shell` once; `takeone doctor` prints its identity in the `motion-shell` row) for `--engine legacy`.

1. `TAKEONE_DIR=<scratch>/takes node bin/takeone.mjs motion resources/demo/tidewater-board.png --out <scratch>/takes/motion --title "Plan the launch with Umer" --device browser --no-ai`. Exit 0; `storyboard.json` records `planner: {by: "local", ...}`; `out/motion.mp4` probes 1920x1080 and `render.json` records `engine: "hyperframes"` plus the pair. `.motion/hyperframes/index.html` has no `file://` or network reference and every `assets/` path resolves.
2. Every pattern: rerun on the same screen with `--pattern hero-reveal,zoom-tour,end-card,kinetic-type`, and once with three or more screens and no `--pattern` (each with a `--region ...:label`) for the launch film with its per-screen type beats and the bento recap. `clip` and `statement` scenes come only from a storyboard (`--storyboard <file.json>`, the shape of `resources/demo/film/storyboard.json`): a `statement` needs only its `title`, a `clip` needs the storyboard's `clips` pointing at real takes, and an optional `audio` bed is copied in and muxed once. `--device phone` frames a screen in the phone frame. Image planning by a model is off (`src/motion/ai.ts` holds the vocabulary for when redaction exists), so every run is planned by the local rules.
3. Real footage that fills the frame: a storyboard whose `clips` point at a rendered real take and whose `clip` scene sets `fill: true` plays that take edge to edge, with its label on an ink chip inside the 5% inset that leaves after its reading time. After the render `out/scenes/` holds one MP4 per scene and `out/scenes.json` lists each one's file, start, end, title and frames; the frames add up to the film's. Render the same storyboard into a second folder and run `node scripts/motion-determinism.ts <a>/out/<id>.mp4 <b>/out/<id>.mp4`: it prints PASS when every frame is within 50 dB PSNR of its twin.
4. `node bin/takeone.mjs render <scratch>/takes/motion --engine legacy --aspect portrait` writes `out/motion-1080x1920.mp4`; `--stills` writes PNGs at the times asked.
5. Look at the frames: decoded settled holds and mid-move frames show the board whole in its browser frame, the title readable and inside the 5% inset, and no word or control cut by a close-up edge. Type beats reveal whole words: decode the first five frames of every kinetic-type, statement and hero scene and confirm no glyph is cut by a mask (the render's safe-area gate also fails on a word a clipping box cuts). Each scene file opens and ends on picture: `ffmpeg -i <scene> -vf "select=eq(n\,0)+eq(n\,N-1),signalstats,metadata=print:file=-"` must not show YMAX-YMIN under 12 (the render refuses such a clip); a black cell at the end of a `tile` contact sheet is padding, not a frame.
6. A change to this path also needs the visual pass's motion cells (`motion-dark`, `motion-light`): `done-done run visual-reference` on the base checkout, then `done-done verify visual` on the candidate.
7. Capture: a still from each shape to $DONE_DONE_EVIDENCE_ROOT/takeone-motion-after.png (side by side) and the ffprobe JSON to $DONE_DONE_EVIDENCE_ROOT/takeone-motion-after.json.

The drive changes no repo file: note findings in the run's report.

## How to get to it (user POV)

The user has screenshots, an HTML page, a URL or an existing take directory and wants a launch film without recording: `takeone motion design.png --out <dir> --title "Ship your next idea" --device browser`. It writes a take-shaped folder (`take.json`, `sources/`, `storyboard.json`, `out/<id>.mp4`, `render.json`) through the default HyperFrames engine; `takeone render <dir> --engine legacy --aspect portrait` re-lays the same timeline and `--stills 1.5,8.5` writes PNGs (the HyperFrames path rejects `--aspect` and `--stills`). With three or more screens and no `--pattern` the local rules plan a launch film (kinetic type, one beat per labelled screen and the end card); the bento recap needs at least three screens labelled with `--region ...:label`. No screenshot is sent to a planner.

## Driving it

Harness: Shell (bash), ffmpeg/ffprobe, `fm-mem-gate.sh` for every render. Needs the pinned HyperFrames pair (`node bin/takeone.mjs motion install-hyperframes` once; network + npm) for the default path, and the pinned headless shell (`node bin/takeone.mjs motion install-shell` once; `takeone doctor` prints its identity in the `motion-shell` row) for `--engine legacy`.

1. On a real screenshot with synthetic seeded text, run `node bin/takeone.mjs motion <image.png> --redaction-dry-run --out <review-dir>`. It writes one redacted PNG and `redaction-report.json` with class/box/length/pass entries, never the original text; open the PNG, check padded whole-line black masks and compare with the local original. The same sensitive detectors re-run for up to three passes; a residual-sensitive failure leaves the preview and report locally, but exits nonzero. No film or planner call occurs. A PNG without recognized text or a missing OCR/barcode tool stops before reporting a clean preview. Keep those previews private.
2. `TAKEONE_DIR=<scratch>/takes node bin/takeone.mjs motion resources/demo/tidewater-board.png --out <scratch>/takes/motion --title "Plan the launch with Umer" --device browser --no-ai`. Exit 0; `storyboard.json` records `planner: {by: "local", ...}`; `out/motion.mp4` probes 1920x1080 and `render.json` records `engine: "hyperframes"` plus the pair. `.motion/hyperframes/index.html` has no `file://` or network reference and every `assets/` path resolves.
3. Every pattern: rerun on the same screen with `--pattern hero-reveal,zoom-tour,end-card,kinetic-type`, and once with three or more screens and no `--pattern` (each with a `--region ...:label`) for the launch film with its per-screen type beats and the bento recap. `clip` and `statement` scenes come only from a storyboard (`--storyboard <file.json>`, the shape of `resources/demo/film/storyboard.json`): a `statement` needs only its `title`, a `clip` needs the storyboard's `clips` pointing at real takes, and an optional `audio` bed is copied in and muxed once. `--device phone` frames a screen in the phone frame. Image planning by a model is off (`src/motion/ai.ts` holds the vocabulary for when redaction exists), so every run is planned by the local rules.
4. Real footage that fills the frame: a storyboard whose `clips` point at a rendered real take and whose `clip` scene sets `fill: true` plays that take edge to edge, with its label on an ink chip inside the 5% inset that leaves after its reading time. The chip sits in the first corner or edge spot where no page text runs under it for its reading time (the composer decodes the scene and measures text under each spot); when page text crosses every spot, the stderr warns `page text crosses every spot the label ... could sit in` and the clip plays framed with its label above the footage instead. Decode frames at 4 fps across such a scene and confirm the label never sits on a heading or line of page text. After the render `out/scenes/` holds one MP4 per scene and `out/scenes.json` lists each one's file, start, end, title and frames; the frames add up to the film's. Render the same storyboard into a second folder and run `node scripts/motion-determinism.ts <a>/out/<id>.mp4 <b>/out/<id>.mp4`: it prints PASS when every frame is within 50 dB PSNR of its twin.
5. `node bin/takeone.mjs render <scratch>/takes/motion --engine legacy --aspect portrait` writes `out/motion-1080x1920.mp4`; `--stills` writes PNGs at the times asked.
6. Look at the frames: decoded settled holds and mid-move frames show the board whole in its browser frame, the title readable and inside the 5% inset, and no word or control cut by a close-up edge. Type beats reveal whole words: decode the first five frames of every kinetic-type, statement and hero scene and confirm no glyph is cut by a mask (the render's safe-area gate also fails on a word a clipping box cuts). Each scene file opens and ends on picture: `ffmpeg -i <scene> -vf "select=eq(n\,0)+eq(n\,N-1),signalstats,metadata=print:file=-"` must not show YMAX-YMIN under 12 (the render refuses such a clip); a black cell at the end of a `tile` contact sheet is padding, not a frame.
7. A change to this path also needs the visual pass's motion cells (`motion-dark`, `motion-light`): `done-done run visual-reference` on the base checkout, then `done-done verify visual` on the candidate.
8. Editorial recipe (model-planned): `node bin/takeone.mjs motion resources/demo/tidewater-board.png --recipe editorial --pitch "<two lines on what it does>" --logo Tidewater --url tidewater.app --out <scratch>/takes/editorial` with the user's signed-in CLI on the normal HOME (never copy its login). Exit 0; stderr names the planner; `storyboard.json` records `planner: {by: "ai"}`, theme `editorial`, scenes `word-swap, phone-story, chip-stack, canon, statement, end-card`, every `at` on the 0.5 s grid. Look at single frames: the struck hook word and the settled word, the phone whole inside the frame (its top and bottom edge both visible) with a lens card beside it that shows the tapped row at reading size (at least 4% of the frame height per line) and the tap ring on the named row, each chip a crisp cut of a real board line and the chip stack filling its side of the frame (no small chips in empty space), one warm accent colour (the screen's own warm hue, or the recipe's orange-red) on the rule under the side copy, the strike line, the lens border and the tap ring, the four canon tiles each replaying a beat, the payoff and the end card, none clipped at the 5% inset. A tap line too long to read in the lens makes the planner ask again (`tap names a line too long to read in the phone's lens`). `test/motion-editorial.test.ts` gates the whole phone, the lens text size, the chip area and the accent on every frame of the real demo board. The same command with a clean `HOME`, isolated `XDG_CONFIG_HOME`, `TAKEONE_NO_KEYRING=1` and a node-only PATH exits 3 with one `takeone needs an AI engine` line and writes no storyboard.
9. Bento recipe (model-planned): `node bin/takeone.mjs motion resources/demo/tidewater-board.png --recipe bento --pitch "<two lines on what it does>" --logo Tidewater --url tidewater.app --out <scratch>/takes/bento`, normal HOME, then render through the render slot. Exit 0; `storyboard.json` has scenes `bento-reel, statement, end-card` on the 0.5 s grid and `shot1..` crops. Time plan + render and report it. Look at frames during the reflows (4.0-4.7 s and 8.0-8.7 s, not only settled ones) and settled ones (1, 3, 6, 10, 11.5 s): five tiles fill the grid with no hole or overlap, headline whole with the highlight wipe, the op-art swaps, the counter and chart, feed rows whole, the real board crops sharp, nothing clipped. The no-engine HOME exits 3 with the sign-in line and writes no storyboard.
10. Existing take: after `takeone make <take> --no-ai` has rendered a real take, `node bin/takeone.mjs motion <take> --out <scratch>/takes/film --title "..." --cta "..."` (through the render slot) exits 0 with no planner call; `storyboard.json` records `source.kind: "take"`, `planner.by: "local"` and scenes `statement, clip, statement` with `clips.footage` over the take's `out/<id>.mp4`. Edit the clip's `start`/`d` by hand, run `node bin/takeone.mjs render <scratch>/takes/film`, and confirm the film follows the edit (new duration) with `planner` unchanged. Look at the frames: the title, the take's UI edge to edge with nothing clipped at the 5% inset, and the closing line. Run `scripts/motion-determinism.ts` on two renders of the same storyboard.
11. Output gates: after a HyperFrames render `render.json` holds `gates.frozen` (`passed`, `longest`, `per_30s`, `runs`) and, with an audio bed, `gates.loudness` (`integrated`, `true_peak`, `lra`). Render a storyboard with `audio` (a music file at `volume` 0.4) and read both; `passed` is true and `integrated` is in -19..-14 LUFS. Extreme case: set `audio.volume` to 1 with a loud file, and the render exits 1 naming the cause; a scene held still outside the end card prints a frozen-time warning and `gates.frozen.passed` is false. Confirm the frozen spans by decoding frames at the span's start and end: they must look identical. `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` carries the motion-video-kit MIT notice.

Recipes and the newer patterns: `node bin/takeone.mjs motion scripts/e2e/scene.html --recipe feature --region "320,180,520,520:Plan the work" --region "872,180,520,420:Track progress" --region "1424,180,520,420:Ship it" --out <scratch>/takes/feature` (also `--recipe social` portrait by default, `--recipe launch --logo NAME`, and `--recipe compare --state before="wait 100" --state after='click #newTask; wait 600' --subtitle "<delta line>"`; `--aspect landscape|portrait` on any). Exit 0 and the probe size matches the aspect. Look at frames inside each stop and the settled summary: the lit hole on its region, the numbered card and connector, the wipe line mid-sweep and at its 50% split with both corner chips, the tilted device mid-turn; nothing outside the 5% inset (the safe-area gate fails the render otherwise). `test/motion-recipes.test.ts` plans every recipe in both aspects.

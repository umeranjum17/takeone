# Change the look and shape of a film on rerender

Part of the takeone verification map. Source: `src/themes.ts`, `src/camera/defaults.ts`, `src/render/edits.ts`, `src/render/framing.ts`, `src/render/stage.ts`, `src/render/pace.ts`, `src/render/safe-area.ts`, `src/render/render.ts`, `src/cli.ts`, `scripts/theme-proof.ts`. Full recipes (aspect, format, cuts, speed, held path): `docs/verification/rerender-look-tweaks.md`, `docs/edit-controls.md`, `docs/look.md`.

## How to get to it (user POV)

The user has a planned take and wants another look or shape without planning again: `takeone render <take-dir> --theme paper`, `--set key=value` for any token in `src/camera/defaults.ts`, `--aspect landscape|portrait|square|4:5`, `--resolution 4k`, `--format mp4|gif|webm|prores4444`, plus `cuts`, `speed` and `zooms` saved in `take.json`. Rerendering never calls the planner.

## Driving it

Harness: Shell (bash), ffmpeg/ffprobe, `fm-mem-gate.sh` for every render. Copy the planned board take from [Plan and render a recorded take](plan-and-render-a-recorded-take.md) first: `render` overwrites `out/<id>.mp4`.

1. `node bin/takeone.mjs render <copy> --theme paper`. Exit 0; stdout ends in `out/<id>.mp4`; ffprobe reports 1920x1080.
2. Look at the film: whole frames during camera moves and settled holds show the board in the paper theme with no word or control cut at a frame edge and the title inside the 5% inset.
3. Another shape: a take that carries `text-boxes.json` (every board scene take) is checked by the safe-area gate, so `render <landscape board> --aspect 4:5` exits 1 with `safe area: N text box placements cross the 5% inset` and writes `safe-area.json` instead of a film. That refusal is the expected result. Capture the board in the shape you export (`node scripts/headless-scene.mjs <dir> --aspect=portrait` or `--aspect=square`) and render it with the matching `--aspect`; ffprobe reports 1080x1920 or 1080x1080, and `cropdetect` reports the full frame (no letterbox). A recorded take without text boxes crops to fill at any export narrower than its source; the camera moves on wide exports and on crop-to-fill `portrait`, `4:5` and `square` exports (`moving` in `src/render/render.ts`), and pans to a click or scroll the planned shot cannot hold (`pressShots` in `src/camera/solver.ts`; see [the re-aim proof](prove-portrait-square-and-4-5-exports-re-aim-on-every-act.md)). Manual `zooms` move the camera on every shape, phone and same-shape exports included (see [the manual zoom proof](prove-a-manual-zoom-arrives-and-holds-on-every-shape.md)).
4. The default (midnight) stage is near-black with a soft accent-hue glow and fine grain. Check it for banding in every plane, not only luma: the stage is generated in Node with a per-channel dither (`backgroundPixels` in `src/render/stage.ts`), and a hue ramp bands in chroma first. Cut one second of a settled hold, upscale each plane to luma size (`extractplanes=y+u+v`, then `scale` to the frame size) and run libvmaf's `cambi` on each as gray (on the board film at 37 s the old blue-to-violet stage measured Y 0.003, U 2.5, V 8.5; the near-black stage Y 0.007, U 2.3, V 3.0, where chroma CAMBI reads grain rather than contours). Then view a stage strip as a 10x high-pass (the pixel minus a 15 px box mean): the old stage shows diagonal colour streaks, the current one even grain only. Look at a whole frame too: the card sharp, centred and inside the frame.
5. For formats, cuts and speed follow `docs/verification/rerender-look-tweaks.md`: per-aspect sizes, the GIF loop marker, bt709 tags on WebM and 4K, and the cuts and typing speed-up checks.
5a. Alpha export: `node bin/takeone.mjs motion scripts/e2e/scene.html --alpha --title "Ship it" --out <dir>` (through the gate) writes `out/motion.mov`. `ffprobe` reports `prores`, profile `4444`, pix_fmt `yuva444p12le`; composite a frame over magenta (`ffmpeg -ss 4 -i out/motion.mov -f lavfi -i color=c=0xff00ff:s=1920x1080 -filter_complex "[1][0]overlay=shortest=1" -frames:v 1 x.png`) and look: magenta shows around the title and device, which stay opaque. A recorded take's `--format prores4444` is opaque by design. Source: `src/motion/hyperframes.ts` (`alphaCoverage`).
6. A change to this path needs the visual pass: `done-done run visual-reference` on the base checkout, then `TAKEONE_VISUAL_REF=<that run's folder> done-done verify visual --outcome "..."` on the candidate (spends judge calls; waits at its gate for approval).
7. Capture: one still from the rerendered film to $DONE_DONE_EVIDENCE_ROOT/takeone-look-after.png and its ffprobe JSON to $DONE_DONE_EVIDENCE_ROOT/takeone-look-after.json.

The drive changes no repo file: note findings in the run's report.

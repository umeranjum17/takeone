# TakeOne 0.5.0

Install TakeOne with `npm install -g takeone-axi`, or from this release:

```
npm install -g --allow-remote=all https://github.com/umeranjum17/takeone/releases/latest/download/takeone.tgz
takeone doctor
```

## What is new

- **Sound in the film.** Click ticks, narration and music that ducks under the voice are mixed into rendered films.
- **Alpha export.** `--alpha` writes ProRes 4444 motion films with a real alpha channel. GIF output now steps size and frame rate down to stay bounded.
- **Smoothness matrix.** `scripts/verification/smooth-matrix.ts` renders every source at every export shape and measures the absolute smoothness bars; `docs/quality.md` records the 14-cell result.
- **Editorial launch film.** `takeone motion <screenshot> --recipe editorial --pitch TEXT` lets the signed-in engine plan the copy and beats. With no engine it exits 3 with the sign-in line.
- **Captions and labels.** AI captions are held to their reading time. The planner chooses spotlight, keycaps and click rings. Labels stay off the page text they would cover.
- **Camera.** Manual zooms now move the camera on every export shape. Tapped controls are framed in portrait and wide phone exports.
- **Cleaner frames.** Titles and captions stay in the band in every export shape. Zoomed text stays clean. Launch films no longer clip words, show blank edges or hold dead air.
- **Score loop.** Each improvement is checked against the solved film, and the loop stops when no gain is left.
- **Screenshot privacy.** Sensitive screenshot masks can be inspected locally before image planning.
- **Engine first.** Default planning now requires a discovered engine and refuses early without one.

## Test these first

1. `takeone motion <screenshot> --recipe editorial --pitch "..."` and check the film's sound: click ticks, narration, and music ducking under the voice.
2. Export the same motion film with `--alpha` and open the ProRes 4444 file over a coloured background to confirm the transparency.
3. Render one take portrait, square and 16:9, and check that titles and captions sit in the band and the tapped control stays in frame.
